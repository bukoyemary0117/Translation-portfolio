# Táyélolú | English–Yorùbá Translator

Bukoye Taye Mary, also known as Táyélolú. I translate between English and Yorùbá, and I care about getting the meaning right,
not just the words.

## About me
I graduated with First Class honours in Linguistics and Nigerian Languages (Yorùbá) from Osun State University, Ikire Campus. I also hold an NCE in English and Yoruba, a TRCN teaching certification,and a UNESCO-IFCD certificate in Digitalising Yoruba Oral Art.

I have taught English and Yorùbá in schools
and I have done English-to-Yorùbá translation work at Neoform AI.
I also run "Yoruba Gems with Táyélolú", where I share Yorùbá language and culture content.

## What I translate
- Academic and formal texts
- Everyday and educational content
- Creative writing, proverbs and oral art

## My approach
- I keep the tone marks and underdots in everything I write.
- I translate meaning, not word for word. Proverbs and idioms are explained so they still land with the reader.
- I read my work aloud before I send it.
